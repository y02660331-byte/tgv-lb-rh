Maine 6th class ke baad aaj tak ek bhi H.W. nahi kiya tha, par tumne hug ka lalach deke mujhse first time H.W. karaya. 😭😂 Tumne kaha tha ki tumhe kiss pasand nahi hai, par maine tumse bet lagayi thi ki main tumhe kiss ke liye mana lunga. Wo baat alag hai ki main tumhe mana kar bhi apni baat chhod deta, kyuki main wo kabhi nahi karta jo tumhe pasand nahi tha. ❤️ Chuttiyon mein hamari bahut ladai bhi hui, but yk, we love each other, to hum wapas ek saath aa jaate the. ❤️ Tumne kaha tha ki gaon jaake exercise karni hai, to maine bahut mehnat ki aur mera weight 49 se 55 ho gaya. 💪 Isi beech main Delhi gaya. Lekin ye khushi zyada time tak nahi tiki. Usi ke agle din, matlab 14 Aug ko, tumhe aunty aur uncle ne pakad liya. Isi beech woh Kanishk wala kand bhi ho gaya tha. Uske baad tumhari jo halat hui hogi, woh main soch bhi nahi sakta. 😔

Maine tumhari halat pata karne ke liye tumhari dost se bhi bola tha. Aur fir hamari 1 week tak baat nahi hui.
